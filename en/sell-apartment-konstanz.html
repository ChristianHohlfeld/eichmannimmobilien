Selling a condominium in Konstanz

Selling an apartment raises different questions from selling a house: what about the owners' association, the maintenance reserve and planned work on the common property? Buyers ask exactly these questions. That is why we put these documents together from the start, so buyers can decide quickly.

First we talk about your goal and time frame. With a property valuation we set a realistic asking price. Then come photos, the exposé and marketing on this website, on our Immowelt profile and to registered buyers. Viewings only take place with checked buyers, and Helmut Eichmann accompanies you up to the notary appointment.

If the apartment is let, we approach investors specifically; the tenancy passes to the buyer. If you live in it yourself, we agree viewings and the moving-out date with you. Selling a whole house instead? Read selling a house in Konstanz. An overview of all services is also available.

Frequently asked questions

Which documents do I need to sell an apartment?

Land register extract, declaration of division, floor plan, living area calculation, energy performance certificate, the economic plan, the latest service charge statements and the minutes of the owners' meetings. If the apartment is let, also the tenancy agreement.

Can I sell a let apartment?

Yes. The tenancy agreement remains in place and passes to the buyer. Let apartments mainly appeal to investors.

What is my apartment worth?

We clarify this with a non-binding market assessment on site. Location, condition, floor, view and the situation of the owners' association all play a role.

Do I need the property manager's consent?

That depends on the declaration of division. Some require the manager's consent to a sale; we check this with you in advance.
