Frequently asked questions

What does a real estate agent in Konstanz actually do?

The agent assesses the market price, prepares documents and the exposé, approaches suitable buyers, organises viewings and accompanies the negotiation and the notary appointment. At Immobilien Eichmann, Helmut Eichmann does this personally.

How much does the agent cost?

When you buy, the buyer's commission is stated in the exposé of each property; New-build Allmannsdorf is commission-free for buyers. For sellers, the terms are discussed openly in the first conversation, before any agreement is made.

Which documents do I need to sell?

Usually the land register extract, floor plans, living area calculation, energy performance certificate and, for apartments, the declaration of division and the minutes of the owners' meetings. We clarify together what is missing. These documents are in German.
