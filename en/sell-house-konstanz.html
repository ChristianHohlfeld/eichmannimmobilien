What matters when you sell a house in Konstanz

Selling a house is rarely routine. Often it is a parental home, an inheritance or a move. That is why every sale starts with a calm conversation about your goal and your time frame. If you cannot yet judge the price, a property valuation in Konstanz is the first step.

After that we take care of photos, the exposé and approaching buyers – on this website, on our Immowelt profile and through direct contacts. Viewings only take place with buyers who have been checked in advance. You decide who you sell to.

If your house has more than one residential unit, also read our page on multi-family houses in Konstanz. Selling an apartment instead? See selling an apartment in Konstanz.

The sale in five steps

  1. First conversation: goals, time frame and key details – by phone or on site in Konstanz.
  2. Market assessment: location, condition and demand.
  3. Preparation: photos, exposé and key data for portals and direct buyers.
  4. Buyers: filtering enquiries, coordinating viewings, collecting feedback and offers.
  5. Completion: support up to the notarial certification of the contract.

Please note that official documents in Germany – land register extract, energy performance certificate, declaration of division, minutes of owners' meetings and the notarial purchase contract – are in German.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to sell a house in Konstanz?

That depends on location, condition, price and documents. We only give a serious estimate after the viewing, not before.

Do I have to renovate before selling?

Usually not. Tidying up and fixing small defects is often enough. Whether a larger measure is worthwhile is discussed after the viewing.

Which documents do I need?

Land register extract, cadastral map, floor plans, living area calculation, energy performance certificate and proof of modernisations. We help to obtain missing documents. These documents are in German.

Can I sell my house while I still live in it?

Yes. Viewings are arranged with you, and the moving-out date is set in the purchase contract.
