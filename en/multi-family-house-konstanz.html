What to check before you buy

For a let building, the tenancy agreements, the list of tenants and the service charge statements are as important as location and condition. Ask about modernisations, the energy performance certificate and the condition of roof, heating and pipes. Helmut Eichmann puts the available documents together for you and organises the viewing. Official documents in Germany – land register extract, energy performance certificate, declaration of division, minutes of owners' meetings and the notarial purchase contract – are in German.

Do you own a multi-family house yourself and are thinking of selling? Then everything starts with a property valuation; the process is described under selling a house in Konstanz. Apartments for sale are listed under buying an apartment in Konstanz.
