What the valuation is – and what it is not

Our assessment is based on local comparisons and current demand. Helmut Eichmann looks at your house or apartment on site and places location, condition and demand in context. You then receive a comprehensible price range that works as the basis for a sale.

Important: this is an estate agent's market assessment for selling and brokerage. It is not a formal valuation report (Verkehrswertgutachten under § 194 BauGB) and not a certified expert service. For court, tax or financing purposes, please commission an independent chartered surveyor.

Methods in brief

  • Comparison approach: derived from comparable properties in a similar location, size and condition – typical for apartments in Konstanz.
  • Income approach: based on sustainable rental income, for example for let properties or investments.
  • Cost approach: land value plus building costs, less age and condition – often relevant for single-family houses.

Frequently asked questions

Is the valuation a formal appraisal?

No. It is an estate agent's market assessment for selling and brokerage, not a formal valuation report. For court, tax office or bank, please commission a chartered surveyor.

What do you need from me?

Address, living area, plot size, year of construction, floor plans and details of modernisations. Anything you do not have to hand we clarify at the appointment.

Do I have to sell with you afterwards?

No. The market assessment is non-binding.

Do you also value apartments and multi-family houses?

Yes – houses, apartments and multi-family houses in Konstanz and around Lake Constance.
