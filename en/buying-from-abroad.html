Good to know

  • Prices: all purchase prices are in euros.
  • Contract: a purchase in Germany is concluded before a notary.
  • Property transfer tax: 5.0 % of the purchase price in Baden-Württemberg (source: service-bw).
  • Commission: shown for each property. New-build Allmannsdorf is commission-free.

For individual legal or tax questions, please consult the notary or your tax adviser. Helmut Eichmann answers your questions about the properties personally.
